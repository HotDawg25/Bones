Bones

A collection of weird little web games.

💀
Physics Live

Super Skeleton Sandbox

A physics playground where you ARE a skeleton. Fight zombies, pilot a submarine to Plastic Beach, discover secret achievements, and unlock gear as you explore a massive hand-built world.
🗿
Shooter Live

Quack of Death

A retro FPS with a twist — you're a moaiduck. Defend yourself against Moaiduck mafiosos and survive as long as Humanly possible(or duckly possible in this case) can in this doom inspired arena shooter.
⛏️
Mining Live

Crappy Cave Explorer

In this game youre a red cube progressing an infinitley generating cave, you can mine, and you can build,What else needs to be said? oh yeah, theres also other miners in there and skeletons.
🌳
RPG Live

Tree RPG

A kid with a stick discovers Grandma's backyard bush is secretly an ancient, evil tree warrior. Turn-based combat, three minibosses, and a twist ending. 📱 Mobile-friendly (on-screen D-pad).
🐢
Casino Live

Turtle Races

Click your way to victory or bet on the field. Name your turtle, buy accessories, and watch out for random chaos on the track. 📱 Mobile-friendly.
🗿
Open World Live

Cobre Valley

Top-down chaos in a small town with a Moai Mart, an off-limits factory, and mischievous duck-moai hybrids. Play as 4 unlockable characters. 📱 Mobile-friendly (virtual joystick).
😴
Horror-lite Live

Go To Sleep

You just need to fall asleep. Watch the closet, under the bed, and the hallway - check what's wrong before it's too late, but don't run out of courage. 📱 Mobile-friendly (tap-only).
📦
Horror Live

The Box

A side-scrolling escape from a bio-containment bunker. Restore power at 3 terminals, drag your box along behind you, and hide when the Subject gets close. 📱 Mobile-friendly.
📦
Sim Live

Fulfillment

Pack and ship orders on a tight deadline. Grab the right box, time the peanut pour, seal it with tape, and keep supply stocked - orders get stranger as the week goes on. 📱 Mobile-friendly.
